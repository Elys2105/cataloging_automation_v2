# 0.1.8 - Pagination, Vietnamese OCR and abstract rules

## Fixed

- Inventory always returns to page 1 and follows every available next-page control.
- Playwright `wait_for_function` now passes `arg=` as a keyword for pagination and submit verification.
- Submit verification tolerates navigation context destruction and verifies DocView/ListDocument independently.
- Viewer/PDF text is preferred before OCR.
- Low-quality OCR receives a second adaptive-threshold pass.
- PaddleOCR and Tesseract results are merged line-by-line; accented Vietnamese lines win over corrupted high-confidence lines.
- Corrupted Vietnamese is blocked from auto-submit.
- Công văn: abstract starts below `Số ...-CV/...` and stops before `Kính gửi`.
- Every non-Công văn type: abstract starts below the document-type heading and ends at the dashed separator. A strong body opener is accepted only when OCR omitted the thin separator line.
- Added conservative phrase-level OCR repairs for the reported Thông báo 82 pattern.

## Operational note

Create a new `Chạy toàn bộ` job after applying this patch. Do not resume Job 15 because its inventory was created before the pagination repair and some submissions were recorded as uncertain.
- Rows already marked `Hoàn thành` by the website are stored as completed and are not submitted again after an uncertain older job.
